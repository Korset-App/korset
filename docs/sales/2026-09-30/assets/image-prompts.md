## Сцена у полки

Файл: `hero.png`.

Create a premium editorial advertising photograph for Körset, a grocery-store digital assistant in Kazakhstan. Landscape 3:2 composition, no text. A close low-angle view down a small clean neighborhood grocery aisle, a shopper's hand holding an unbranded carton at lower center-right, fresh produce, shelves with deliberately non-legible neutral packaging, a muted lavender tote visible. Bright natural window light, warm cream and soft lilac palette, contemporary serious European editorial retail photography, tactile real materials, subtle film texture, impeccable authentic anatomy. Keep the left third uncluttered and softly lit. No branding, no legible labels, no barcodes or QR codes, no fabricated phone interface, no corporate handshake or smiling portrait. This is an illustrative scene, not a customer case study.
